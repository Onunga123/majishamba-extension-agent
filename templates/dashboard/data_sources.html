Market prices

Market price signals are ingested from public market information sources and officer field reports. No automated API is currently configured for market data.

Agronomic guidance

KALRO maize manual ingestion is permission-pending. See the guidance page for the bibliographic record, licence basis, and the open KALRO publication page.

Synthetic data disclaimer

All household, plot, weather, pest and market records in this repository are synthetic. No real household names, plot coordinates, phone numbers, or yields are used. Locality names in Kachieng’ Ward are confirmed by the project owner but no government classification is claimed. Synthetic test scenarios appear on this page only when DEMO_MODE is on (development) — they are hidden in production.
