Data & Sources

Weather, pest, agronomic and system sources

The system does not auto-fetch from KMD, KALRO, or KEPHIS (no public APIs exist). As an authorized extension officer, you manually read the official publication, then transcribe its metadata into the forms below. The system records full provenance (authority, publication date, validity period, coverage, source URL) and labels each record honestly.

🌦 Ingest KMD bulletin

Transcribe metadata from a Kenya Meteorological Department forecast you've read on meteo.go.ke. Records the bulletin as a WeatherSignal with verification_status = current_official (or regional_context for Lake Victoria forecasts).

 🐛 Submit officer field report (pest)

Record a verified local pest observation from your own field visit. Labelled as officer_field_report — NOT as a KEPHIS/KALRO publication. Your name is recorded as the author.

 📋 Ingest published pest notice

Transcribe metadata from a published official pest notice (KEPHIS alert, county agricultural report, etc.). Severity is stored EXACTLY as published — do not inflate.
